# Portfolio design contract

Preserve charcoal, copper, sand evidence panels, DM Sans, Instrument Serif, the portrait, résumé, and contact form. Pale teal and two orbital points give the opening a restrained futuristic character.

## Narrative

1. Reliable data. Clear decisions. Explicit BI Engineer, Data Analyst, and Business Analyst positioning.
2. Professional impact and the résumé/repository provenance statement.
3. Direct commerce, revenue, and finance project links, with work-in-progress/build-specification labels. Detailed retention, claims quality, experimentation, and hospital-price evidence follows. One link to the twenty-project directory.
4. Three role-specific capabilities and a three-line biography. Healthcare is genuine experience, not the boundary of the portfolio.
5. Existing contact form, untouched.

Data Analyst is the current employment title in the hero, biography, and structured metadata. Target-role labels do not change the actual job title. Keep the linked résumé consistent; do not claim LinkedIn was edited without authenticated access.

## Motion and rendering

Prerender full HTML. Hydrate only the contact form. No rotating headline, animated charts, scroll progress, scroll listeners, per-frame geometry reads, or Framer Motion. One IntersectionObserver may add is-visible; content starts visible and does not animate on reveal.

Only two 8px satellite points animate, using CSS transform. Orbital paths and typography stay still. The native checkbox pauses the points without JavaScript. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. No backdrop filters. Transition/keyframe properties are limited to transform and opacity.

Below-fold sections use content-visibility: auto and contain-intrinsic-size: 800px; printing restores visibility. Charts remain visible with JavaScript disabled. Preserve contact-preserved.css and Contact.jsx.

## Evidence

Claims: 2,791 clean + 98 quarantined = 2,889 ingested; 98 of 98 planted defects isolated.

Prices: 93.75% charge-description mapping accuracy, 45 of 48 against a gold crosswalk.

Retention: 45.3% versus 18.2%; activation means creating a project within three days; association is not causation.

Experiments: 120 versus 6 false positives in 500 simulated tests; 24.0% versus 1.2%.

Preserve “Test data, not employer production work,” “Association, not proof of cause,” and “not savings achieved by a hospital.” Never substitute project metrics for employer outcomes.
